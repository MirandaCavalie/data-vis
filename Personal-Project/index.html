Displaced

Nearly Three Decades of Eviction in San Francisco

In 1997, San Francisco began requiring landlords to file eviction notices with the Rent Board. What followed was a record of displacement: over 48,000 notices spanning tech booms, housing crises, a pandemic, and the reshaping of entire neighborhoods. This is that story, told through data.

    About This Project

    Eviction data comes from the SF Rent Board Eviction Notices dataset published by DataSF, covering 48,000+ notices filed between 1997 and 2026. Rent figures are HUD Fair Market Rents (2-bedroom) for San Francisco County, sourced from HUD User.

    Dataset: SF Rent Board Eviction Notices via DataSF
    Rent data: HUD Fair Market Rents, San Francisco County (FIPS 06075), FY2006–2025
